Initial qualification target:

    examples/hello-raster/main.asm

Results are classified as:

- `MATCH` — PRG files are byte-for-byte identical.
- `MISMATCH` — both assembled successfully, but bytes differ.
- `UNQUALIFIED` — one or both assemblers could not build the source.

A MATCH demonstrates agreement for this artifact only. It does not establish complete SceneASM compatibility.

Run:

    make differential-hello

The driver preserves assembler-specific PRGs and symbol files under `build/differential/` for inspection.
