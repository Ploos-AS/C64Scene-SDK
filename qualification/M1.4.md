# M1.4 — Headless VICE qualification harness

M1.4 makes emulator execution reproducible without weakening the qualification contract.

## What the harness proves

A successful headless invocation may promote an artifact from **BUILD** to **RUN** when the scripted emulator path completes.

It does **not** by itself promote a raster routine to **QUALIFIED**.

## Outputs

`tools/vice/qualify-double-irq.sh` writes:

- `build/qualification/double-irq-vice.log`
- `build/qualification/double-irq.result`

The result is deliberately machine-readable. Until raster/cycle measurements are extracted and checked, it records:

    status=RUN
    timing=NOT_MEASURED

Missing/incompatible VICE records an unqualified result instead of manufacturing a PASS.
